## Hero

**Compassionate & Professional Counselling in Glasgow – Daryl MacDonald**

Hi, I'm Daryl MacDonald, a dedicated and compassionate counsellor offering both
in-person and online counselling sessions. With 5 years of experience, training
in person-centred approaches, I provide a safe, non-judgmental space where you
can explore your thoughts and feelings at your own pace.

## How I Can Help

Do you ever feel stuck, repeating the same patterns of behaviour again and
again? Do you ever wonder where you are going wrong, making choices that don't
reflect the life you want to live? Do you ever feel there's no one to share
these thoughts with, afraid of burdening the ones you love?

You are not alone with these feelings - in today's fast moving world, we don't
make the time to slow down and really look at our lives, to understand how we
can change and move forward. That's what I offer you - a space to reflect, to
understand yourself better and to build the life you deserve.

## What I Offer

- **Flexible Sessions** – Choose between in-person sessions at my office in
  Glasgow City Centre, or secure online appointments that fit your schedule.
- **Personalised Style** – Every individual is unique, and so is my approach. I
  tailor sessions to your specific needs and goals.
- **Structured Support** – We will work together to set goals and milestones to
  help you track your progress.
- **Safe & Confidential** – Your privacy and security are my top priorities.
