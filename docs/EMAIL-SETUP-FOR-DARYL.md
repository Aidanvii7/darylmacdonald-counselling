# Moving your email to Gmail — what's happening and what you need to do

Hi Daryl,

Your website has already moved to its new home and is live at
**darylmacdonald.com**. The last thing still sitting with your old provider is
your **email address**, `daryl@darylmacdonald.com`.

We want to move it to **Gmail**, so it works like a normal Gmail inbox but keeps
your own address. It costs about **£5–6 a month** for the one mailbox.

## The bit that matters most

When your old provider's plan ends, that email address **stops working** —
anything sent to it will bounce, and any messages still stored there are gone
permanently. We don't know the exact end date; it may be the end of this month.
So this is worth doing sooner rather than later.

You mentioned you don't need your old messages, which keeps this simple. Just be
sure: **once the plan ends, they can't be recovered.** If there's any chance
you'll want old client emails or receipts, tell Aidan and we'll copy them to your
computer first.

## What you'll need to do

1. **Sign up for Google Workspace** at `workspace.google.com` — choose *Business
   Starter*, say you already own `darylmacdonald.com`, and create your
   `daryl@darylmacdonald.com` address. You'll enter your own card details and
   pick a new password.

2. **Send Aidan two things.** During signup Google will show you some settings
   (a verification code, then some mail settings). You can't change those
   yourself — they live in Aidan's account — so just copy them over to him and
   he'll put them in. He'll tell you when each one is done.

3. **Test it.** Send an email from your new Gmail to a friend and get them to
   reply, so we know it's really working before we consider it finished.

4. **Update your phone and computer.** Remove the old email account from your
   mail apps and sign in with Google instead.
